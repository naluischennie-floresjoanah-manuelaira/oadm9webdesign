🎓 Bachelor of Science in Office Administration (BSOA)

📌 About the Course

A 4-year degree program that provides training in office management, business communication, records management, computer applications, and administrative support. It prepares students to become efficient, organized, and competent professionals who can handle daily operations in any type of office environment.

📚 Key Topics

  • Office Management & Procedures
  • Business Communication & Correspondence
  • Records & Information Management
  • Computer Applications (MS Office & Productivity Tools)
  • Work Ethics & Human Relations
  • Administrative Support Services
  • Basic Accounting & Bookkeeping
  • Event Coordination & Office Protocol

💡 Why It Matters

It is the backbone of every organization: it ensures smooth daily operations, improves productivity, maintains professional standards, and supports decision-making. No business or institution can function effectively without skilled administrative staff.

💼 Career Opportunities

  • Office Manager
  • Administrative Assistant
  • Records Officer / Clerk
  • Front Desk Officer
  • HR Assistant
  • Data Entry Specialist
  • Office Staff Supervisor
  • Virtual Assistant / Admin Support Specialist

✨ Why Choose BSOA?

It’s not just about typing or filing — it’s about being the reliable support system of the business. It is a practical, in-demand skill set that opens opportunities in almost every industry, from government offices to private companies and even remote work.
